# Methods and limitations

## Read each evidence domain separately

**Recorded data:** motion analysis uses a measured reference and a particular sample. A lower recorded-reference error need not establish better anatomical truth. Results from the same exposed development session do not establish prospective generalization or population benefit.

**Sensing engineering:** device acquisition, timing and software interfaces are useful engineering results. Geometric validity and the interpretation of a depth measurement need their own evidence. A returned frame is not a validated joint position.

**Robot simulation:** bounded SO-101 task development uses MuJoCo and controlled scenes. The reviewed manipulation experiments still rely on simulator ground-truth contact checks and controlled scene assumptions. Passing a development task does not prove a research method outperforms an ordinary baseline. Generated-command interface checks also distinguish HOLD acceptance from simulated motion and settling; they do not establish instantaneous stopping, real-time performance or human-study benefit.

**Physical operation:** real cameras observing a person do not show a robot executing that person's intention. No complete physical-loop advantage or safety claim is made here.

## Comparison discipline

Simple baselines matter. Development and formal evaluation must remain distinct; unsuccessful comparisons should not be hidden by a favorable subset. Forecasting, preparation, authorization, action application and observed outcome are different claims. This portfolio does not join separate historical tests into a single purported end-to-end result.

## Disclosure discipline

Unpublished research implementations, detailed method combinations, participant records, raw motion/video, unpublished comparisons and manuscript figures are absent. The architecture SVG is newly drawn for this overview. There is no robot video or synthetic performance chart.

## Planned evaluation

The next scientific questions concern independent measurement validity, strong baseline comparisons, robust task realization and separately authorized physical-loop evaluation. These are research directions, not completed milestones or guaranteed future results.
