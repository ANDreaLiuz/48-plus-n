# How to read the V6 illustrations

This visual edition changes the presentation, not the scientific evidence. The new illustrations are original AI-assisted vector artwork made for this portfolio. No photo, participant image, research frame, robot CAD, measured trajectory or manuscript figure was reused.

| Illustration | What it explains | What it is not |
|---|---|---|
| Human / signal / embodiment hero | Human authority and the two research roles | A photograph or a tested physical system |
| Sensing plate | Generic RGB-D instrumentation context | A real capture, calibration record or geometric accuracy finding |
| Forecast plate | A non-numeric comparison question | Measured trajectories, an observed future or an asserted advantage |
| Simulation-domain plate | A bounded manipulation/re-observation context | A MuJoCo experimental frame, upstream model or physical robot photograph |
| Authority map | Public-level conceptual roles and separate evidence streams | A private controller specification or complete physical-loop result |

The artwork uses new SVG paths, shapes and typography. It encodes no original measurements or unpublished mechanisms. Captions distinguish media type from evidence domain: a real-sensing research record can be discussed beside a conceptual drawing without turning that drawing into evidence.

Original research remains independent work by ANDreaLiuz. Upstream software and robot resources keep their existing authorship and rights. The portfolio uses AI assistance for presentation; no general reuse license has been selected.
